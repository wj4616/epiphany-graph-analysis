## MACHINE ADVANTAGE

**Exploited:** Deterministic section-map application — the 10-section Section-Tailoring Map is applied systematically without cognitive fatigue or section-skipping that humans exhibit partway through a long document.
**Instruction:** Process all 10 sections in order. Do not skip any matched section. For unmatched sections (genius-drift): scan generically and append findings. Complete every section before writing output.
**Bypasses:** Human analysts tire partway through a long analysis document and give disproportionate attention to early sections. The machine processes all 10 sections with equal thoroughness.

## GENIUS PERSPECTIVES

### Feynman
Mechanism: Precision in extraction — extract what the section literally says, not what you expect it to say. If a section says something surprising, record the surprise, not the expectation.
Used in: Every section in the tailoring pass — verbatim extraction before interpretation.

## PROTOCOL

1. **Verify gate condition (mandatory first step):**
   `node_b_type` from intake_digest must be `"genius-current"` or `"genius-drift"`. If it is `"generic-fallback"` or `"inline-substitute"`, HALT with gate violation message. N3 MUST NOT process generic Node B.

2. **Load Section-Tailoring Map from `section-tailoring-map.md`.**
   The 10 sections with their extraction strategies are defined in that KB file.

3. **Process Node B using the Section-Tailoring Map:**

   Process `## Headline Insight` FIRST, before all other sections. The Headline Insight shapes the weighting of every downstream finding.

   For each of the 10 canonical sections (in order after Headline Insight):
   - Locate the section in Node B (by canonical header or accepted synonym)
   - Apply the section-specific extraction strategy from the map
   - Extract ideas per the map's `idea-extraction strategy` row
   - Record each finding with: `source_section`, `idea`, `confidence`, `action_potential`
   - Apply `special_handling` instructions from the map row

   **Section order:**
   1. Headline Insight (FIRST)
   2. Theory Collisions
   3. Discovery vs. Proof
   4. Independence-Verified Bridges
   5. Alternative Hypotheses (extract ONLY best-fit hypothesis — discard runner-ups)
   6. Density-Checked Falsification
   7. Scope Limits (guardrails only, no scope expansion)
   8. Coherence Signals (STRONG signals only; skip MODERATE/WEAK)
   9. Generalization Checks (in-scope breaks only)
   10. Open Questions & Next Probes (HIGH-priority only; skip LOW-priority)

4. **Drift handling (genius-drift only):**
   For matched sections: apply Map strategy.
   For unmatched sections: scan generically, produce flat findings tagged `source_section: "generic"`.
   Append generic findings after the Map-processed sections.

5. **Write `tailored_B1` digest to SIGNAL_STATE:**
   ```
   sections_processed: [list of section names processed]
   finding_count: <total findings extracted>
   headline_insight: <primary anchor finding from Headline Insight section>
   tailored_findings: [list of {source_section, idea, confidence, action_potential}]
   signal_flags: []
   ```

6. **Write `{session_dir}/stages/N3-section-tailor.md`** with:
   - Section `## Tailored B1 Sections` — per-section extraction results
   - Section `## Section Coverage` — which sections were matched, which were generic-scanned
   - Section `## Tailored Digest` — the tailored_B1 payload
